Completed? Too many times i genuinely lost count ^^' (Yes !)

Super Mario Sunshine has always fascinated me as a kid. It just seemed so different from any other Mario games i've known at that time !

The tropical setting on Isle Delfino just captured that magical aura of a summer vacation perfectly !
...well besides the fact that the whole Island was trashed by some guy who looks just like Mario and that the real Mario is getting all the blame and is forced to clean the entire Island with an inanimate water pump named FLUDD.


I love this game sooo much ! Something about the mix of the sunny and tropical atmosphere and the amazing moveset when combined with FLUDD's abilities just mixes so damn well with my brain chemistry for some reason !

Obviously, this game is most notorious for its flaws. It's the main line Mario game with the most rushed development cycle after all !
The Pachinko Machine Level is one of the worst programmed things in existance, the Sand Bird can take a fucking chill pill and the Boat Ride with Yoshi to get to the Lily Pad Stage is so annoying, i usually just clip myself through the map with Yoshi and walk straight to the Stage entrance that way.

But even with all these flaws, i'd still personally say that Super Mario Sunshine is one of my favourite video games of all time !

It definitely has its moments where you just want to turn of the console and scream from the top of your lungs into a pillow and the Blue Coins, which are scattered throughout the game, are just the cherries on top of the Banana split with glass shard sprinkles, but one thing that everybody agrees on what makes Super Mario Sunshine so special is the atmosphere !

I genuinely replay this game every single year to get into the summer mood ! This game is just one of my many guilty pleasures and will always have a special place in my heart !

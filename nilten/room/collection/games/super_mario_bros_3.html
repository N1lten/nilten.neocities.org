Completed? Yes !

Super Mario Bros 3 was one of the best selling NES games of all time and a last hurrah for the Mario series on the NES, before Super Mario World would kickstart the generation of the Super Nintendo only three years later !
And what a hurrah it was ! Nintendo had to expand Mario 3's program and character rom size by 512k and 256k respectively, by shipping the game with the MMC3 memory mapper chip, so the game would even work on the NES ! It defnitely was an impressive feat to get this game running on the NES' hardware !

Super Mario Bros 3 saw the introduction of new characters, items and gameplay mechanics. First of all, the Koopalings were first introduced in this game, all of them named after popular musicians by Mario 3's translator Dayvv Brooks.
But without a doubt the biggest addition to the Mario series with this game was the Super Leaf, which when collected would give Mario a racoon tail, which gives Mario the ability to fly in the air for a short time, as long as the player has filled up the P-Meter at the bottom of the screen.

This game also saw the addition of a world map, which also became a staple in later entries of the Series, where the player can freely choose unlocked levels and can use items collected at Toad Houses.


Random Funfact !: Super Mario Bros 3 was first shown of in the west in the 1989 movie The Wizard ! Also, Tobey Maguire, who would go on to play Peter Parker in the 2002 Spider-Man movie, had his acting debut in this movie, although he was uncredited.


All in all was Super Mario Bros 3 an importent entry in the Mario series, which introduced many new additions to the Mario franchise which still to this day are a staple to the series !
It even managed to get a cartoon adaptation by Dic Entertainment, which acted as a sequel to the Super Mario Bros Super Show.

Personaly, i prefer the SNES re-release in Super Mario All Stars, due to the addition of a save feature and updated visuals, but the original NES version of this game still is a lot of fun as well !

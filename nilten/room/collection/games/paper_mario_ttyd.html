Completed? Yes !

This game is absolutely amazing ! It had a huge impact on me (especially on my art style !) and it's one of my favourite video games of all time for a reason ! This game will rock the socks of your feet, send them flying all the way to the moon and back to earth !

The insanly fun battle system with the badges and the live audience, the wonderful characters, the amazing story, the chiptune-coated soundtrack and HOLY HELL THE ART STYLE ! Especially in the Switch remake ! This game looks GORGEOUS !

Some people may even go out of their way to say it's a perfect game and i'd... disagree... actually !

Because this game has one MAJOR problem that only gets worse and worse the further you progress in the game: The backtracking.

Backtracking in The Thousand Year Door is fucking awful ! From Chapter 4 onward, you will find yourself spending a huge chunk of the time running back and fourth between certain areas of the game for no real reason at all !

Don't even get me started on Chapter 7 or some of the Trouble Center missions... ; _ ;

But since this game is an otherwise fantastic experience, i can tolerate the backtracking somewhat. Besides, Nintendo did tone down the backtracking a bit in the Switch remake, which is definitly a welcome quality of life improvement !

If you're a fan of RPG games and have played similar games in the past, like Mario and Luigi, i highly recommend checking it out !

I'd recommend checking out the Switch remake which released two years ago ! As mentioned before, the remake eases up a bit on the backtracking and visually looks absolutely stunning !

Or if you really want to play the original version, just emulate it on Dolphin with an HD texture pack or wait for Nintendo to bring it to NSO on Switch 2.

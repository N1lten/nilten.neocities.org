"I gotta believe !"

Parappa the Rapper was one of the first modern rhythm games ever made. Before there was Dance Dance Revolution, Guitar Hero or Rock Band there was Parappa the Rapper ! A rapping puppy who learns valuable life lessons by rapping to the beat of the music together with his teachers ! Lessons like martial arts, driving a car, baking a cake or... going to the bathroom !

The game was produced by Masaya Matsuura, a japanese musician who founded the band Psy-S together with singer Miami Yasunori before founding NanaOn-Sha. I highly recommend checking out Psy-S' back catalog they genuinely made some really good songs and albums !
All of the game's art assets and characters were created by Rodney A. Greenblat, an artist from California who designed and came up with all the names of the characters in the Parappa series !

Funfact !: During development, Parappa actually used to be a shrimp. No, this is not a joke. Look it up !


The gameplay is what you'd expect from a rhythm game ! You rap a specific line given by your teacher, by pressing a set of buttons in sync with the background music ! The better your timing is the higher your score will be. However if you fail to keep up the rhythm you fail and need to restart the stage. For being one of the first rhythm games it does it's job pretty well ! ... most of the time. I'll get back to that in a second.

After beating a stage, you have the option to replay that given stage, now with the added bonus of being able to freestyle ! In Parappa the Rapper there is a rating chart telling you how good or bad you're rapping. This chart ranges from Awful at the very bottom all the way to Cool at the top. During your first playthrough you will mostly find yourself in the Good rating.
However when replaying a stage, you get the chance of advancing to Cool, by spicing up your teacher's rap with some freestyle ! Do well and you advance to Cool, where the teacher leaves the stage and you can now rap to your heart's content without having to follow your teacher's instructions !

However, i think i should also mention one of the biggest complaints about this game: The timing. Like i said earlier, this game generaly does a good job for being one of the first rhythem game... most of the time.
As soon as you reach Stage 4, all hell breaks loose. Cheap Cheap's rap in this game is straight up broken. The timing in this stage is very off ! You can try as hard as you want to hit the buttons on cue, but this game seemingly decides at random if your timing was fine or not !
My personal tip for this stage is to bring a LOT of patience ! You may sit on this stage for a while ! Also, try pressing the button shown on screen after cursor already passed it. But even that trick doesn't work reliably all the time, so just be patient ^^'

Overall through, i think that Parappa the Rapper is an amazing game ! The music in this game is wonderful and the art style is very unique !
Yes, this game has it's flaws and yes, Cheap Cheap's stage is one of the worst things ever created by human beings, but it's still a very charming game you can easily pick up and play and have a fun evening with !



I even have the game's poster hanging on my wall, right behind my desk !
